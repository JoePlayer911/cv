    How to Play Hanzidle
    
      
    Guess today's secret vocabulary word within 6 tries. Type pinyin, meaning, or hanzi into the box and pick a suggestion (or press Enter when there's exactly one match).
    
      
    Every guess compares against the secret word with 4 clues:
    
      
      
        
    • Length — 🟩 same number of characters, ⬆️ the secret word is longer, ⬇️ the secret word is shorter.
      • 
        
    • Initial — 🟩 same pinyin initial sound, ⬛ different initial.
      • 
        
    • A–Z Order — 🟩 you found the exact word, ⬆️ the secret word's pinyin sorts later alphabetically, ⬇️ it sorts earlier.
      • 
        
    • Meaning — 🟩 the English meaning has the same word count, ⬆️ the secret meaning has more words, ⬇️ fewer words.
      • 
      
    
      
    A new puzzle unlocks every day (UTC) for each level, and everyone gets the same word.
