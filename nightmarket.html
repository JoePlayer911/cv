How to play

  • Customers sit at your counter and order a word. Serve the matching dish from the kitchen pass before their patience ring runs out.
  • Tap a dish, then tap the customer — or drag the dish onto them.
  • Day 1 orders show the meaning, Day 2 adds tone-coloured pinyin, Day 3 brings 🔊 listening orders and 👑 VIPs, Day 4 the grumpy 🧐 Food Critic.
  • Fast, correct serves build a combo and bigger tips. Lose a customer and you lose a ⭐ — run out and the stall closes.
  • Spend coins each evening on upgrades. Survive 7 nights to become a Market Legend.
  • Keyboard: 1–8 pick a dish, Q W E R T serve a seat (or replay a spoken order), Esc pause.
