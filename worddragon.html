How to play
  • Steer with arrow keys / WASD, swipe on the board, or the on-screen pad. Space / P pauses.
  • Eat the characters in order. Each one joins your dragon's body.
  • A wrong or out-of-order character costs a heart and the dragon coughs up two segments. Walls and your own tail cost a heart too.
  • Golden 龙珠 dragon pearls give slow-mo, pinyin reveal, a magnet or an extra heart.
  • Spell words with no mistakes in a row to build a streak multiplier.
